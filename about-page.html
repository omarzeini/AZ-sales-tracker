About AZ Sales Tracker

AZ Sales Tracker was created By Omar Zeini for the Restaurnat AfroZone when it was just getting started.

We needed a simple way to track the restaurant's performance without spending money on a POS terminal. We'd already spent most of our budget getting the restaurant set up, so buying another piece of software wasn't really an option.

But running a business without knowing what you're actually making is difficult. We needed answers to simple questions:

How much did we make today? Which dishes are selling the most? Which ones aren't worth keeping?

And we didn't just want to know this at the end of each day. We wanted to see how things were performing week after week.

That's where AZ Sales Tracker came in.

I'm the brother of AfroZone's founder, Mohamed , and since I was already a web developer, I decided to build the tool ourselves.

And it turned out to be more useful than we expected.

It helped us identify dishes and juices that were costing us money without bringing enough sales, while showing us which products were bringing customers back. We were able to make those decisions without spending anything on expensive software.

After seeing how much it helped AfroZone, we realised that other small businesses could benefit from the same kind of tool. restaurants, cafés, retail shops, and even online stores.

So I decided to polish it, turn it into a proper product, and make it available to anyone who might find it useful.

hope AZ Sales Tracker helps you understand your business a little better. But I also hope your business grows enough that you eventually need a proper POS system. Hhhhh. 😂

And if you're running an online-only business, you might not need one at all.
