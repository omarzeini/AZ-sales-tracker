Guide of use

AZ Sales Tracker helps small businesses track their stores performance for free. Businesses can create accounts add their products and record sales whenever they sell a product. the app automatically calculates and shows total sales made in a day in details. displays most sold and least sold products. revenue and gross profit. It also generates weekly reports of sales.

01

Create Your Account

Start by creating an account. Once you sign in, You will be redirected to the home page where you can see daily sales and revenue.

02

Add Your Products

You can click on the add sale tab and click the "add sale" button at the top right of the page. Or by navigating to the business Tab, when clicking add product you will get a Model/Form that cantains Product Name, Price and Cost. fill these fields with your product details and click Save. the product cost is used to automatically calculate the Gross Profit for you.

03

Record a New Sale

When you sell a product. simply navigate to the add sale page if you're not already there. And click on the products dropdwon list (It says click to select), Then select a product from the list. After that increase the quantity to the number of items you sold of that product then just click on "Add Sale" button beneath.

04

Monitor Daily Revenue

Visit the Home Tab to see your sales in the day and the total revenue you generated form them.

05

Review the Summary Page

The summary page brings together your core business numbers in one place. that includes: top sold products, least sold products, your total revenue and total gross profit.

06

Follow Weekly Insights

Use the weekly insights page ( you can navigate to it by clicking the "Weekly Insights" button at the top right of the summary page) to understand how your sales evolve across the week. This helps you recognize best-selling products, stronger sales days, and opportunities to improve your strategy.

07

Manage Your Business

The business Tab displays your business information like its name and email.and help you manage your products by editing, deleting or adding new ones. each prodcut card has two icons at the bottom a pen icon for editing it and a trash icon for deleting it. you can leverage the search bar to find a specific product.
