About AZ Sales Tracker

AZ Sales Tracker was created By Omar Zeini for the Restaurnat AfroZone when it was just starting. We needed a software to track our business performance without spending money on POS Terminals because we had already spent too much on the restaurant kit, and were really low on budget.

It's impossible to run a restaurant without knowing what your store is generating and from what exact products, or in our case dishes. The aim was to know: How much did we make today ? What are the dishes that sell best?. Not only on a daily basis but on a weekly basis too.

That's why Omar(me) The brother of AfroZone founder Mohamed, Decided to build this tool: AfroZone Sales Tracker, or AZ Sales Tracker for short. Since i was already a Web Developer.

And it was worth it, It helped us in a way we didn't even think it would, We could get rid of some dishes and juices that were only a wast of money and focused on some others that were the only reason customers actually came back, Without spending a penny.

Realising how much it helped us, we figured that there could also be some startup stores like restaurnats, retail shops, small cafes or even an online business that could use this tool and save a lot of time and money.

So I decided to polish it a bit and publish it so that any-one out there can use it.

I really hope it help you in your business, but i also hope your business grows enough that you get a POS Terminal, Hhhhh , If you are running an online only store it could really come in handy, Because you shouldn't need a POS Terminal.
