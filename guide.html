Guide to Using AZ Sales Tracker

AZ Sales Tracker helps small businesses track their store's performance for free. Businesses can create an account, add their products, and record sales whenever they make a sale.
The app automatically calculates and displays your daily sales in detail, including your most- and least-sold products, revenue, and gross profit. It also generates weekly sales reports to help you understand how your business is performing over time.

01

Create Your Account

Start by creating an account. Once you sign in, You will be redirected to the Home page, where you can see daily sales and revenue.

02

Add Your Products

You can add a product from the Add Sale page by clicking the "Add Sale" button at the top right of the page.

Alternatively, navigate to the Business tab and click "Add Product". A form will appear where you can enter your product's name, price, and cost.

Fill in these fields with your product details and click "Save". The product cost is used to automatically calculate your gross profit.

03

Record a New Sale

When you sell a product, simply navigate to the Add Sale page if you're not already there.

Click the product dropdown menu labelled "Click to select", then select a product from the list.

Next, increase the quantity to the number of items you sold, then click the "Add Sale" button below.

04

Monitor Daily Revenue

Visit the Home tab to see your sales for the day and the total revenue generated from them.

05

Review the Summary Page

The Summary page brings your core business numbers together in one place.

Here, you can see your top-selling products, least-selling products, total revenue, and total gross profit.

06

Explore Weekly Insights

Use the Weekly Insights page to understand how your sales change throughout the week.

You can access it by clicking the "Weekly Insights" button at the top right of the Summary page.

This helps you identify your best-selling products, your strongest sales days, and opportunities to improve your business strategy.

07

Manage Your Business

The Business tab displays your business information, such as its name and email address. It also lets you manage your products by editing, deleting, or adding new ones.

Each product card has two icons at the bottom: a pen icon for editing the product and a trash icon for deleting it

You can also use the search bar to quickly find a specific product.
